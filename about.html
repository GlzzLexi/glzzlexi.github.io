FOUNDER & VISION

THE STORY OF GLZZLEXI

GlzzLexi Studios is an independent game studio founded and led by John Gabriel (Gabriel Ronao). The studio began as a solo-operated indie effort, engineering modular Luau modules covering data persistence, gameplay frameworks, and development tooling for Roblox.

While John remains the solo developer today balancing his 3rd year in Computer Engineering in the Philippines, GlzzLexi was architected from day one to scale into a specialized, multidisciplinary game company.

The Standalone PC Vision: Expanding into standalone desktop gaming is our natural next chapter. By taking systems-driven gameplay from Roblox into standalone 3D executables distributed directly on glzzlexi.me, we completely bypass the $100 Steam fee and platform gatekeeping.
