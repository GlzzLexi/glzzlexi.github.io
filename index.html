STUDIO ORIGIN & VISION

BUILT BY JOHN GABRIEL,
ENVISIONED FOR A TEAM

GlzzLexi Studios was founded and is led by John Gabriel (Gabriel Ronao), a 3rd-year Computer Engineering student.

It started as an independent venture born from late-night coding between university engineering subjects, circuit analysis, and Luau script architecture. Today, GlzzLexi operates independently, but with the stated objective to scale into a specialized team of developers, technical artists, and scripters.

We treat game design with engineering discipline: modular systems, clean data persistence, and responsive mechanics that respect player time.
