About Me

Roblox to Desktop

I'm a 3rd year Computer Engineering student making games in my spare time.

Most of the games I make right now are on Roblox. Making games on Roblox taught me a lot about player engagement and making mechanics responsive. Now I want to challenge myself by building a complete standalone 3D first-person desktop game.

Because Steam requires \$100 per game just to submit, I'm sticking to releasing it right here on this website. Once the winning game is ready, you'll be able to buy and download it directly from here (supporting local payment methods like GCash / Maya as well as Card/PayPal).
