BEHIND GLZZLEXIBEAR

BUILT FROM INDIE PASSION,
SCALING FOR DESKTOP GLORY

GlzzLexiBear was founded with one clear ambition: create games that make you smile the second you boot them up, and keep you sweating because the gameplay is so thrillingly tight.

Starting as an independent studio experiment, we honed our design instincts by making punchy, responsive experiences. Today, our eyes are set firmly on desktop PC gaming. Modern PC gaming gives independent studios the greatest creative freedom on earth — direct distribution via Steam, dedicated enthusiast communities, and hardware capable of rendering stunning art in 4K at 144 frames per second.

"We don't do boring corporate game loops. If it doesn't have sharp wit in the design and sweet glaze in the feedback, it doesn't leave the studio."

— Founder, GlzzLexiBear Studios
